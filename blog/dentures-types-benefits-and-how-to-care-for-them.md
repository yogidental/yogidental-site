### Understanding the Different Types

Every smile is unique, which is why artificial teeth are never one-size-fits-all. Depending on how many teeth you need to replace, your dentist will generally suggest one of three practical paths:

-   **Full Dentures:** Built for people who need to replace a whole upper or lower row of teeth. They rest directly on your gums and stay in place using natural suction or a thin layer of dental adhesive.
-   **Partial Dentures:** Ideal if you still have plenty of strong, healthy natural teeth left. These fill in individual gaps and lock into place with subtle, tooth-colored clips.
-   **[Implant-Supported Dentures](/dental-implants):** Anchored right into your jawbone with small titanium screws. This setup offers the ultimate stability, so you never have to worry about shifting, clicking, or slipping while eating.

* * *

### Key Benefits for Your Health and Life

Getting your teeth restored does a lot more than just fill in empty spaces on a photo. It makes a real difference in how your body functions every day:

-   **Easier Eating:** You can go back to enjoying a full diet of fresh, solid foods without struggling to chew.
-   **Clearer Speech:** Empty gaps often cause whistling noises or slurred words. Filling those spaces lets you speak naturally again.
-   **Better Facial Structure:** When teeth go missing, facial muscles lose support and can make your cheeks look sunken. Restorations hold your face's natural shape.
-   **Protected Alignment:** Partials block surrounding natural teeth from slowly leaning or shifting into empty spots over time.

### How to Care for Them Daily

Keeping your new smile clean does not take much time, but sticking to a simple routine makes all the difference:

-   **Rinse Off Food:** Take a few seconds to run your appliance under warm water after meals to wash away loose bits of food.
-   **Brush Wisely:** Use a soft-bristled brush made specifically for dental appliances. Standard toothpaste can actually scratch the surface, so stick to mild cleansers or simple soap.
-   **Soak Overnight:** Your restoration needs to stay damp so it does not lose its shape. Leave it in plain water or a mild soaking solution while you sleep.
-   **Wipe Your Gums:** Gently clean your gums, tongue, and the roof of your mouth every morning before putting your appliance back in.
