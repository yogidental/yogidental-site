Many people feel they smile with their mouth closed because of one small detail. Their teeth look short, and their gums seem to take over the picture. Brushing, whitening, and straightening cannot fix a gum line that sits too high or sits unevenly. Gum contouring can. It is a simple way to reshape the gum line so the teeth look balanced, and it is one of the most underrated ways to change how a smile looks.

* * *

### What Gum Contouring Actually Is

Gum contouring, also called gum reshaping, removes or sculpts small amounts of gum tissue to create a smoother, more even gum line. The goal is not to change your teeth. It is to reveal the natural length and shape they already have.

Dentists use it for a gummy smile, for gum lines that sit higher on one side than the other, and for teeth that look small only because of extra tissue. Practices that focus on family and cosmetic dentistry often use contouring as a small step that produces a visible difference. * * *

### Why Laser Makes a Difference

Traditionally, this kind of reshaping was done with a scalpel. Today, many practices use a dental laser instead, which is more precise and often more comfortable. A laser can remove tissue and seal the area at the same time, which generally means less bleeding and a calmer healing experience.

Because the work is exact, the dentist can shape each tooth's gum line carefully and check the result as it takes form. Your dentist will explain what to expect based on your case.

* * *

### Who Is a Good Candidate

Gum contouring is not for everyone, so a proper exam comes first. Good candidates often include people who:

-   Show a lot of gum when they smile
-   Have an uneven gum line that makes some teeth look shorter than others
-   Feel their teeth look small despite normal tooth length
-   Have healthy gums and bone, confirmed by an exam and X-rays

If gum disease, bone position, or tooth length is the real cause of the problem, a different treatment may be a better fit. That is why an honest evaluation matters more than the procedure itself.

* * *

### Gum Contouring and Other Smile Treatments

Contouring often works best as part of a bigger plan. It can set the stage for **[porcelain veneers](/porcelain-veneers)**, **[crowns](/porcelain-crowns)**, or **[whitening](/zoom-teeth-whitening)** by creating even, symmetrical gum lines before other work begins.

### What to Expect Before and After

During a consultation, your dentist will examine your gums, review your X-rays, and discuss how much change is realistic. Mild soreness or sensitivity can happen as the area heals, and your dentist will give you simple care instructions for eating, brushing, and rinsing. Healing time and results vary from person to person, so the best way to know what applies to you is to ask directly.

* * *

### Questions to Ask Before Treatment

-   Is my gummy smile caused by gum tissue, tooth length, or jaw position?
-   Is a laser the right option for my case?
-   Will I need any other treatment to reach my goal?
-   What does the healing process look like?
-   How will the new gum line look with the rest of my smile?

* * *

### Conclusion

A beautiful smile is not only about the shape of your teeth. It also depends on the frame around them. Gum contouring can bring balance to a smile with a quick, precise treatment, especially when it is guided by a careful exam.
